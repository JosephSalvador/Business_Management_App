## Layer Guide (`jolinx_logo_master.psd`)

The master Photoshop document is organized into **16 distinct layers**, enabling multimedia creators, motion designers, and video editors to easily isolate elements for 3D parallax, UI animations, and print materials:

1. **Canvas Base (`01`)**: Solid background canvas.
2. **Presentation Mat (`02`)**: Clean studio mat surrounding the badge.
3. **Squircle Container (`03`)**: Beveled rounded-square container.
4. **Specular Bevel (`04`)**: Top-edge ambient glass refraction.
5. **Emerald Neon Glow (`05`)**: Ambient outer luminescence.
6. **Growth Pillar 1 (`06`)**: Short metric bar (early growth).
7. **Growth Pillar 2 (`07`)**: Middle metric bar (acceleration).
8. **J-Hook Loop (`08`)**: Bottom curved arc of letter "J".
9. **Ascending J-Stem (`09`)**: Vertical pillar reaching upward.
10. **Apex Arrowhead (`10`)**: Directional growth peak.
11. **Metallic Core (`11`)**: Specular white highlights.
12. **Ascent Particles (`12`)**: Energy sparks and particle flecks trailing the arrow.
13. **Ambient Particles (`13`)**: Floating luminescence surrounding the icon.
14. **Wordmark (`14`)**: Crisp "JOLINX" typography.
15. **Tagline (`15`)**: "BUSINESS OS" subtitle.
16. **Master Composite (`16`)**: Flat artwork reference.
