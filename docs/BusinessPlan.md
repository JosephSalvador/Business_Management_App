# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## 1.1 Business Overview

**Jolinx Business OS** is a comprehensive Business Operating System designed specifically for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in East Africa. The platform replaces fragmented tools — spreadsheets, paper records, and basic POS systems — with a unified, offline-first solution that handles sales, inventory, accounting, and financial management.

**Legal Name:** Jolinx Technologies Ltd.
**Structure:** Limited Liability Company (Tanzania)
**Headquarters:** Dar es Salaam, Tanzania
**Founded:** 2026
**Industry:** Enterprise Software / SaaS
**Stage:** Pre-revenue, Product Complete

## 1.2 Mission Statement

To empower every African business with affordable, offline-first technology that provides complete visibility into sales, stock, and money — enabling owners to make informed decisions without being physically present at the shop.

## 1.3 Vision Statement

To become the leading Business Operating System for African SMEs, replacing Western ERPs with a platform built from the reality of African commerce — cash + mobile money, intermittent internet, local tax compliance, and Swahili-first interfaces.

## 1.4 The Opportunity

East Africa is home to 45+ million SMEs. 98% operate without digital systems. Existing solutions (Odoo, Zoho, SAP) assume constant internet, single currency, Western tax rules, and English-only interfaces. They are too expensive ($50–$500/user/month), too complex (weeks to onboard), and too fragile (offline = dead).

**Jolinx solves this by being:**

- **Offline-first:** 72-hour offline window with idempotent sync
- **Mobile money native:** M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa as first-class citizens
- **Locally compliant:** Built-in TRA/KRA tax engines, Swahili UI
- **Affordable:** Per-branch pricing from $20/month
- **Simple:** Onboarding in under 10 minutes
